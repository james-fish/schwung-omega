What's new in v0.6

  • Widgets: every page draws the sound — the kick's pitch dive, ghost-kick taps, the GEN necklace with a live playhead, dice, the warehouse with a ping bouncing in it, the sidechain pump.
  • Warehouse reverb: concrete early reflections + an 8-line FDN with grit in the loop. Types: Warehouse, Concrete, Tunnel, Dirt (6-bit, decimated).
  • Sequencer voices: Basic, Detune, FM, Acid Saw, Acid Sq, Dirt, Dirt 2, Bleep, Stab. Each has its own two knobs next to VOICE (e.g. Acid: RES + ENV AMT, with SLIDE on Seq Variation).
  • Drive on the volumes: RUMBLE VOL and SEQ VOL are 100% at noon; past noon they drive into the voice's filter at roughly even loudness.
  • Space: separate sends from the rumble, sequence, kick and delay into the shared reverb, plus a tempo-synced sequence delay (1/16 to dotted 1/4) with feedback and tone.
  • Seq Variation: patterns (Up/Down/Ping-Pong/Leap/Pedal + Offbeat, Rolling, Acid, Call+Resp, Stabs, Octaves riffs), accent, octave jump, ratchet, slide, note-random and LOOP.

Engine: not started. Same C as the Move's dsp.so, compiled to WebAssembly.
